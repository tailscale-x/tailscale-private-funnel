# v4.0.6 — Replacement control-Service discovery

This follow-up to v4.0.5 completes the generated peer-discovery policy. Load
balancers and Docker, Tailnet, and Kubernetes gateways receive ICMP-only access
to their installation's control-role devices. This complements the existing
load-balancer-to-gateway discovery grant and keeps replacement control-Service
hosts visible to clients that were already connected.

Neither grant opens device TCP/UDP ports or grants human management access.
Funnel only generates policy: an administrator must review and merge the rules.
No additional OAuth scopes or listeners are required.

The live main installation's narrow control grant was explicitly approved. A
real control Pod replacement produced a new disposable device; the unchanged
edge process discovered it and connected directly over Tailscale without a
restart, identity replacement, session-refresh hook, or diagnostic-client join.
The DNS ledger, exposure bindings, persistent encryption material, and decrypted
credentials remained unchanged.

See [role-policy instructions](https://github.com/tailscale-x/tailscale-private-funnel/blob/main/docs/TAILSCALE-ROLES.md).
The report, streaming-drain, and Service-listener fixes are included from v4.0.5.
Single-host workload updates can still interrupt traffic; this does not promise
zero-downtime replacement or change Tailscale's coordination service.
