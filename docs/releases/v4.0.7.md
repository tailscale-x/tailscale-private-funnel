  immediate permanent-denial handling, and cancellation. Retry notices remain
  bounded; there is no nested retry-batch loop.

  redundant listener close. Preserve transport-close and assignment-release order.

  from earlier hypotheses and historical deployment notes.

continuity, lease fencing, and guarded Tailscale dialer. No schema, API, routing
policy, endpoint-grace, identity-lifetime, or ACL change is introduced. The pinned
upstream SDK remains; no map-reset or identity-repair hooks are added.
