## Rollout observations and remaining limitation

This was not a zero-downtime deployment. Control replacement reproduced the known approved-Service-host map propagation delay. Native gateways initially had no candidate for the new control Service host; configuration/report calls also timed out while Kubernetes workloads initialized. Harbor and Metube temporarily returned 502. The edge restart temporarily stopped public acceptance while the old process shut down. Existing owned DNS and cached routes were retained throughout; forwarding subsequently recovered.

The edge's running identity later dialed control through the expected userspace Service route and direct UDP at approximately 1 ms. Control snapshot publication recovered and all DLKN routes became Programmed again. Separate client DNS-resolution timeouts were also observed; final public resolver checks succeeded.

At final verification, edge and 5090 native application-readiness checks passed. Tiny's Metube Service was serving and reports were being accepted, but its initial-success readiness flag remained false after repeated control report/inventory timeouts. This is an explicit operational qualification: successful public checks do not establish that control-request latency or the upstream Service-map issue is permanently resolved. No extra reset, fake identity, policy workaround, or weakened ownership check was used to make those checks pass.

Private database backups, identity snapshots, checksums, rollout receipts, HTTPRoute status, public probes and Facebook results are under `/tmp/funnel-v404-rollout-20261020/`; never commit that directory or its secret-bearing contents.
